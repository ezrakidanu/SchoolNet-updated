# SchoolNet assets

**Sole creator, developer & author: Ezra Kidanu**

The SchoolNet logo (`static/images/logo.svg`) is an original vector mark made for the Dessie Special Boarding School (YWMDSBS Dessie) community app. Staff avatars are colored monograms for the illustrative Mr. Abebe, Ms. Sara, and Coach Dan profiles; they do not depict real people.

The PWA icons (`pwa-180.png`, `pwa-192.png`, `pwa-512.png`, and `pwa-maskable-512.png`) are raster exports of the SchoolNet vector mark for touch icons and Android installation.

The Science Club image (`static/images/science-lab.jpg`) was retrieved from the [Unsplash laboratory-science collection](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/laboratory-science) through image search. It depicts a microscope and lab equipment. Confirm the original photograph's applicable license and attribution requirements before commercial redistribution, or replace it with a school-approved image.

The interface uses system font stacks and local static files; it does not fetch remote fonts, scripts, icon libraries, or tracking SDKs. Decorative interface icons are inline SVGs included by the local Jinja macros.
